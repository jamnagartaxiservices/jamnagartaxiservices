Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

This policy explains how Jamnagar Taxi Services ("we", "us") handles information when you visit this website or book a taxi with us.

1. Information we collect

We collect only what you choose to share, such as your name, phone number, pickup and drop locations, travel date and time, and the number of passengers when you call, message us on WhatsApp or book a ride.

2. How we use information

  • To confirm your booking and arrange your car and driver
  • To contact you about your pickup, trip or any change
  • To answer your questions and handle complaints
  • To meet legal, tax or accounting requirements

3. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We share trip details such as your name, phone number and pickup point only with the driver assigned to your trip, and with authorities when the law requires it.

4. Third-party services

This website links to WhatsApp and Google Maps, and shows a Google Maps map on the contact section. When you use them, those services' own terms and privacy policies apply.

5. Cookies and analytics

This website does not set tracking cookies of its own. Embedded services such as Google Maps may set their own cookies.

6. Data security

We take reasonable care to protect the information you share with us, but no method of sharing information is completely secure.

7. Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete the contact details we hold about you by calling 98242 70752 or visiting our office.

8. Contact
