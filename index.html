About us

Your local taxi service, right opposite the S.T. Bus Stand

Jamnagar Taxi Services is a taxi service based in Jamnagar, Gujarat. You can find us opposite the S.T. Bus Stand on Summer Club Road, and you can reach us by phone at any hour.

We offer cars for short city trips as well as longer journeys, with a choice of a sedan or larger cars for families and groups. Tell us where you want to go and when, and we will arrange the right car.

Whether you are a local resident, a visitor arriving at the bus stand, airport or railway station, or a family planning a wedding, we aim to get you there on time and in comfort.
